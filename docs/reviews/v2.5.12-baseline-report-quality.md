## 2. Identified Deficiencies

1. **Deficiency #1 (Service Value Attribution):** The report groups value into generic "Tier-1 Machine vs Tier-2 Analyst" defense rather than the 4 mandatory categories:
   - Microsoft Technology Value
   - KoçSistem Managed Service Value
   - Customer Action Value
   - Shared Outcome
2. **Deficiency #2 (Customer Decision Framework):** The report lacks the four-quadrant decision tables (Approved Decisions, Pending Decisions, Deferred Decisions, Recommended Decisions). C-Level executives cannot discern what decisions they must ratify.
3. **Deficiency #3 (Storytelling Chain):** Metrics do not systematically follow `Evidence -> Meaning -> Risk -> Action`. Numbers are presented without stating the direct risk and who owns the follow-up.
4. **Deficiency #4 (Executive 6-Question Readability):** A non-technical CISO/CEO cannot immediately discern what KoçSistem specifically accomplished versus what Microsoft E5 licenses provided automatically.
5. **Deficiency #5 (Language & Title Mixing):** Period strings mix English and Turkish ("August 2026 Dönemi"), and legacy ASCII question marks exist in legal notices.
