# Executive Readability & 6-Question Evaluation Standard

## 1. The CISO & Board Executive Standard
A customer CISO, CIO, or Board Member must be able to read the report and answer the following **six fundamental questions** within 3 minutes without requiring clarification from a security engineer:

1. **What happened during this reporting period?**
   - Clear summary of fleet health, processed alerts, and active threat vectors.
2. **Why does it matter to our business?**
   - Direct translation of technical telemetry into operational continuity and regulatory protection.
3. **What value did Microsoft E5 technology provide?**
   - Autonomous machine-speed protection, automated playbooks, and platform threat intelligence.
4. **What value did KoçSistem MSSP engineers deliver?**
   - Expert triage hours saved, custom KQL threat hunting campaigns, posture tuning, and false positive elimination.
5. **What residual risks remain in our environment?**
   - Transparent visibility into unpatched CVEs, ghost endpoints, or DLP policy override trends.
6. **What decisions and approvals are required from leadership?**
   - Prioritized decision backlog with owners, business impact, and deadlines.

---

## 2. Rejection Rule
If any generated executive report cannot answer all six questions clearly:
**The report must be marked as REJECTED regardless of successful HTML/PDF rendering, clean code compilation, or automated test pass rates.**
